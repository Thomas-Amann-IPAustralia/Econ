## How to read these numbers

- Each need has one or more gold passages, quoted verbatim from a document. A chunk *covers* a gold passage when it holds at least half of its characters. Gains are novelty-based: a chunk scores only for gold passages that no higher-ranked chunk already covered.
- `recall@4k_tok` is the share of a need's gold passages that reach the answer model when the top chunks fill 4,000 tokens of context. It is fair across chunk sizes; `recall@10` is not, because ten large chunks hold more text than ten small ones.
- `ndcg@10` rewards finding the gold passages near the top. `doc_hit@5` asks only whether a gold document appears in the top five.
- The test set is a silver standard: one author (Claude) wrote each question from a passage, so it measures finding the passage a question was written from. Other passages that also answer it count as misses, which understates every setting alike. Treat small differences as ties.
