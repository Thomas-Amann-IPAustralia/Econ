## How to read these numbers

- Each need's pooled passages (the top 10 of every setting here, plus the judge pilot's top 20 for 12 needs) were graded 0-3 by gemini-3.8-flash against the need's narrative: 3 directly answers a key part, 2 helps answer part, 1 on topic but no use, 0 not relevant. Claude's blind grades of 100 of those pairs agreed with the judge at quadratic kappa 0.92.
- `ndcg@10` rewards highly graded passages near the top of the context (gain 2^grade - 1). The ideal ranking is built from every judged passage of the need, so it is comparable across settings.
- `rel_recall@20` is the share of the need's judged-relevant passages (grade 2 or 3) that reach the answer model's 20 passages. Unjudged passages count as not relevant, so a setting that surfaces unjudged passages is understated; judged@10 shows how much.
- `gold_recall@*` uses the silver set's gold spans, the old yardstick, for silver needs only.
- Dev chooses, test reports. With 92 test needs, differences under about 0.03 are hard to separate.
