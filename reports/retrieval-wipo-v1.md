## The rules (fixed before any number)

1. For the variant chosen in rule 2, IP Australia ndcg@10 over all 114 silver needs falls by no more than 0.02 against ipa-only, and the paired 95% bootstrap interval's lower bound is above -0.04. Otherwise the WIPO documents go to the owner as a separate collection (searched only on request), with the numbers.
2. Among docling, docling-prefix, docling-tables, data and drop, choose the highest WIPO dev ndcg@10 (all WIPO needs). A variant that differs from docling only in the profile pages is preferred to docling when it is not worse on the dev non-profile needs by more than 0.02 and better on the dev profile needs. Ties (within 0.005) go to the simpler variant, in the order docling, drop, docling-prefix, docling-tables, data. Test reported.

## How to read these numbers

- A `quote` gold is covered by a chunk holding at least half its characters; a `facts` gold (an economy-profile figure) by a chunk from that page holding the indicator's name and its value.
- `wipo_share@10`: the share of WIPO passages in a need's top 10. For IP Australia needs it shows the crowding that rule 1 guards against.
- One author (Claude) wrote both test sets from passages, so they measure finding the passage a question was written from. The WIPO set is small (see the counts above): treat differences under about 0.05 as ties.
